Hostel And Day School Facility

Our school provides a safe, secure, and comfortable hostel facility for students. The hostel is designed to create a home-like environment that supports academic excellence and personal development.

Our school offers both Day School and Hostel facilities to meet the educational needs of all students.

  • Medical Assistance
  • Clean and Hygienic Environment
